THE GUARDIAN'S CODEX

Awaken the ancient light.

Your ship fires automatically. Drag in the field to steer, or use arrows / WASD. On touch screens, one finger can steer while another selects a shield.

△ FireAries, Leo, Sagittarius

◇ EarthTaurus, Virgo, Capricorn

≋ AirGemini, Libra, Aquarius

▽ WaterCancer, Scorpio, Pisces

Absorb a matching seal to earn 10 points and 50 charge. Two seals fill your counterattack. Wrong shields cost one hull, followed by one second of protection. Matching absorptions still count during protection.

At full charge, tap Counterattack or press Space. This spends all charge, clears falling seals and damages every enemy. Destroyed enemies earn 100 points each. Invaders that pass out of view give no points.

Survive three 30-second waves, then defeat the final formation. Fire and Water appear first; Earth arrives in wave 2 and Air in wave 3. Short landscape, blur and hidden tabs pause the run; resume manually.
